## Page text

**Latest version:** https://playdeezgames.github.io/enby-jam-2026/ (also add it as an external link on the page; live since November 8, 2026)

**Gwen's Peregrination II** is a menu-driven journey. You are Gwen. There are 2,000 miles between you and the end. You walk them one choice at a time.

You start with ten snax and no jools. Pick yer pace, from Very Slow to Very Fast, and keep going. Walking makes you hungry and tired. Eat snax, forage, take a nap, and don't let yer health reach zero.

Every stretch of road brings something new, or nothing at all:

- a patch of flowers (and Traehi, who loves them, and pays in jools)
- a vending machine that sometimes rips you off
- a Kwik Trip with a fountain of Dr Pepper
- a shrine to a cat
- a sink full of dirty dishes, which you can wash for money
- a shortcut, which might save miles, or might not
- a recycle bin, which matters more than you think
- a DMV, where there is always a line

Litter you leave behind makes shortcuts worse. Litter you recycle makes them better. The road remembers.

New in this version: a DMV with a driver's handbook, a permit test that depends on how well you studied, an instructor who charges by the lesson, and, if you get that far, a license and a car.

Between turns there are ad breaks, because this is a metaphor and metaphors need sponsors.

### Controls

- Keyboard: Up and Down then Enter, or press the number or letter next to a choice. Escape backs out. PageUp and PageDown (or the mouse wheel) scroll long text.
- Touch: tap a choice.
- Yer game saves itself after every choice and picks up where you left off.
- Found a bug that is not a metaphor? Use "Send feedback" under the game, or leave a comment.

### About this version

Gwen's Peregrination II was first written for **Nonbinary Game Jam 2026** in VB.NET. This is a rebuild for the browser in Odin compiled to WebAssembly, with saving, a text-grid screen and the driving lessons added.

**AI disclosure:** this rebuild was written with Claude Code (Claude Sonnet 5.5): the code, the tests and the new texts for the DMV, handbook, permit, instructor and license. The original game, its design and its words are by TheGrumpyGameDev, who was the product owner and tester for the port.
